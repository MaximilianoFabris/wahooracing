Choose a saved fixture, then move its integer sample slider. The mechanism angle changes only the mechanism and geometric port labels. Cylinder and acoustic clocks remain independent. Lines connect saved samples for display; no intermediate physics is calculated. Missing quantities show UNAVAILABLE. This viewer reads saved data and offers no simulation launch control.
